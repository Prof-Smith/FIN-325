# Semester Roadmap

The course is designed as a cumulative analyst-development sequence.

| Module | Focus | Primary Artifact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Financial Management and Analysis | Lab 1 + Memo 1 |
| 2 | Time Value of Money | Lab 2 |
| 3 | Bond Valuation | Lab 3 |
| 4 | Equity Valuation | Lab 4 + Memo 2 |
| 5 | Risk and Return | Lab 5 |
| 6 | Cost of Capital | Lab 6 |
| 7 | Capital Budgeting | Lab 7 |
| 8 | Capital Structure and Financial Risk | Lab 8 + Memo 3 |
| 9 | Strategic Financial Decisions | Memo 4 |
| Final | Valuation Recommendation | Capstone Project |

<div class="callout-gold">
The end goal is not a collection of assignments. The end goal is a professional valuation recommendation built over the semester.
</div>
