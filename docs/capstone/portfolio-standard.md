## Purpose

The capstone should be strong enough to serve as a professional finance portfolio artifact after removing course-specific submission language.

## Portfolio-Ready Criteria

A portfolio-ready capstone should be:

- Clear enough for a non-specialist decision-maker to follow
- Technical enough to demonstrate applied finance competence
- Concise enough to prioritize what matters
- Honest enough to acknowledge uncertainty
- Professional enough to discuss in an interview

## Required Professional Elements

- [ ] Executive recommendation appears in the first paragraph.
- [ ] Company name, ticker, market price, and observation date are clear.
- [ ] Financial health evidence is interpreted, not merely listed.
- [ ] Valuation is compared with market price.
- [ ] Risk and required return are discussed together.
- [ ] WACC is interpreted as a hurdle rate.
- [ ] Capital allocation evidence is included.
- [ ] Strategic finance and stewardship are addressed.
- [ ] Risks and limitations are explicit.
- [ ] Workbook outputs support the written recommendation.
- [ ] Sources are documented.

## Portfolio Editing Guidance

After grading, students may revise the final report for professional use by:

1. Removing course submission language.
2. Adding a short executive summary.
3. Cleaning workbook formatting.
4. Exporting selected tables or charts.
5. Saving the project as a PDF and Excel artifact.
