## Purpose of the Playbook

This playbook helps you move from class assignments to a professional analyst recommendation.

Use it as a checklist while developing the final capstone.

---

## The One-Sentence Test

Before writing the full report, complete this sentence:

```text
I recommend [recommendation] for [company] because [reason 1], [reason 2], and [reason 3], although [key risk].
```

If you cannot complete this sentence clearly, the recommendation is not ready.

---

## Evidence Selection Test

For each piece of evidence, ask:

1. Does this evidence affect the recommendation?
2. Is this evidence more important than other available evidence?
3. Can I explain what the evidence means?
4. Can another reader verify or understand the source?
5. Does this evidence reveal a strength, weakness, risk, or assumption?

If the answer is no, the evidence may belong in the appendix or not at all.

## Recommendation Language Guide

Use precise recommendation language.

| If the evidence shows... | Use language like... |
|---|---|
| Strong financial health, attractive valuation, acceptable risk | Recommend |
| Attractive company but valuation or risk concerns | Recommend with caution |
| Mixed evidence or insufficient margin of safety | Watchlist |
| Weak valuation, excessive risk, or poor flexibility | Do not recommend |

---

## Common Analyst Mistakes

Avoid these common mistakes:

- Starting with a favorite company and forcing the evidence to fit
- Confusing company quality with stock attractiveness
- Treating valuation as one exact number
- Ignoring risk because the company is familiar
- Treating WACC as certain
- Using IRR without comparing it to WACC
- Ignoring downside scenarios
- Listing ratios without interpretation
- Writing a summary instead of a recommendation

---

## Final Review Questions

Before submitting, answer these questions:

1. Is my recommendation clear in the first paragraph?
2. Does each section support the recommendation?
3. Did I compare estimated value with market price?
4. Did I discuss risk and required return together?
5. Did I explain what could make my recommendation wrong?
6. Is the workbook easy to audit?
7. Would a professional reader understand what action I recommend?

---

## Final Standard

The capstone should show that you can use finance to make better decisions under uncertainty.
