## Purpose

Use this template to draft the final investment recommendation report.

## Report Header

```text
To: Investment Committee
From: [Your Name]
Date: [Date]
Subject: Final Investment Recommendation for [Company Name] ([Ticker])
```

## 1. Executive Recommendation

State the final recommendation in the opening paragraph.

```text
I recommend [Recommend / Recommend with Caution / Watchlist / Do Not Recommend] for [Company Name] because [reason 1], [reason 2], and [reason 3]. The key risk to this recommendation is [risk].
```

## 2. Company and Investment Context

Briefly explain what the company does, how it makes money, and why it is worth analyzing.

## 3. Financial Health Diagnosis

Use evidence from Memo 1 and the Financial Health tab of the workbook.

Discuss liquidity, profitability, leverage, DuPont analysis, or other relevant financial evidence.

## 4. Valuation Analysis

Use evidence from Memo 2 and the Valuation tab.

Compare estimated value with market price. Explain whether the stock appears undervalued, overvalued, or near fair value.

## 5. Risk, Required Return, and Cost of Capital

Use evidence from Memo 3, the Risk and Return tab, and the WACC tab.

Explain whether expected return appears adequate for risk and whether the cost of capital affects the recommendation.

## 6. Capital Allocation and Project Discipline

Use evidence from Memo 3 and the Capital Allocation tab.

Discuss NPV, IRR, payback, scenario analysis, or evidence of investment discipline.

## 7. Strategic Finance and Stewardship

Use evidence from Memo 4 and the Strategic Finance tab.

Discuss leverage, coverage, working capital, payout policy, stress testing, and responsible stewardship.

## 8. Risks and What Would Change the Recommendation

Identify the most important reasons the recommendation could be wrong.

## 9. Final Recommendation

Restate the recommendation and connect it to the strongest evidence.
