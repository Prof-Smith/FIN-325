# Lab 1: Financial Diagnosis

## Objective

Use financial statement analysis to evaluate whether a publicly traded company appears financially healthy.

## Build

Create a ratio analysis workbook that includes:

- Liquidity ratios
- Profitability ratios
- Efficiency ratios
- Leverage ratios
- DuPont analysis

## Analyze

Use your results to answer:

1. What is the company's greatest financial strength?
2. What is the company's greatest financial weakness?
3. What is driving ROE?
4. Is leverage helping or hurting the company?
5. Are there any warning signs?

## Decide

Write a 250-500 word recommendation:

> Based solely on your financial statement analysis, would you invest in this company? Why or why not?

## Submission Requirements

Submit through Canvas:

- Excel workbook
- Written analysis
