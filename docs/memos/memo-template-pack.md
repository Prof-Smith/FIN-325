## Purpose

Use these templates to draft the four investment memos. Each memo should be concise, evidence-based, and organized around a decision.

---

## Memo 1 Template: Company and Financial Health

```text
To: Investment Committee
From: [Your Name]
Date: [Date]
Subject: Memo 1 - Company and Financial Health for [Company Name]

Recommendation: I would [continue / continue with caution / not prioritize] [Company Name] as a potential investment candidate because [main reason].

Company Context: [Company Name] ([Ticker]) operates in [industry]. The company is relevant for analysis because [brief reason].

Financial Evidence: The strongest financial health evidence is [liquidity/profitability/leverage/DuPont evidence].

Interpretation: This suggests [what the evidence means for company health].

Concern or Limitation: The main concern is [risk, limitation, or missing comparison].

Conclusion: Based on the initial financial health analysis, I would [decision].
```

## Memo 2 Template: Valuation Recommendation

```text
To: Investment Committee
From: [Your Name]
Date: [Date]
Subject: Memo 2 - Valuation Recommendation for [Company Name]

Recommendation: I would [buy / avoid / place on watchlist / continue analyzing] [Company Name] because [valuation conclusion].

Market Context: [Company Name] ([Ticker]) recently traded at approximately [$X] as of [date].

Valuation Evidence: My valuation analysis produced [DDM/P/E/DCF/range evidence].

Interpretation: This suggests the stock appears [undervalued / overvalued / near fair value].

Assumption Sensitivity: The conclusion depends most heavily on [growth rate, required return, multiple, cash flow assumption].

Concern or Limitation: The main limitation is [valuation uncertainty].

Conclusion: Based on the valuation evidence, I would [decision].
```

## Memo 3 Template: Risk, WACC, and Capital Allocation

```text
To: Investment Committee
From: [Your Name]
Date: [Date]
Subject: Memo 3 - Risk, Cost of Capital, and Project Evaluation for [Company Name]

Recommendation: The risk, cost of capital, and capital allocation evidence [strengthens / weakens / complicates] my investment view because [main reason].

Risk-Return Evidence: The company's beta, expected return, and required return suggest [interpretation].

Cost of Capital Evidence: The estimated WACC is [X%], which means [interpretation].

Capital Allocation Evidence: The NPV, IRR, or scenario evidence suggests [interpretation].

Concern or Limitation: The main limitation is [model limitation or assumption risk].

Conclusion: Based on this stage of analysis, I would [decision].
```

## Memo 4 Template: Strategic Finance Recommendation

```text
To: Investment Committee
From: [Your Name]
Date: [Date]
Subject: Memo 4 - Strategic Finance Recommendation for [Company Name]

Recommendation: The strategic finance evidence [strengthens / weakens / complicates] my long-term investment view because [main reason].

Strategic Context: The key strategic issue is [growth, payout, leverage, expansion, restructuring, or working capital].

Financial Flexibility Evidence: The company's leverage, coverage, working capital, or payout evidence suggests [interpretation].

Stewardship and Integrity: From a responsible stewardship perspective, [interpretation].

Concern or Limitation: The main limitation is [risk or uncertainty].

Conclusion: Based on the strategic finance evidence, I would [decision].
```
