## Welcome to Module 4

Module 4 shifts from promised bond cash flows to uncertain equity cash flows. Students compare dividend, earnings multiple, and cash-flow-based approaches and learn why equity valuation is best understood as a range of defensible estimates.

---

## Essential Question

> **How do analysts estimate the value of a stock when future cash flows, growth, and risk are uncertain?**

---
