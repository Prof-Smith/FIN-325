## Welcome to Module 1

Module 1 establishes the foundation for the course. Students begin with the role of financial management, the goal of the firm, agency conflicts, governance, financial institutions, markets, and the ethical responsibilities attached to financial decision-making.

---

## Essential Question

> **How do financial managers create value, and how does the financial environment shape managerial decisions?**

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

### Primary Outcome

#### LO1

**Describe the role of managerial finance and the financial market environment.**

### Integrated Prior Outcome

This module also draws on earlier or related course outcomes so students can connect individual finance tools into broader financial judgment.

- **LO8:** Responsible Stewardship and Integrity are introduced through agency relationships, governance, and the goal of the firm.
