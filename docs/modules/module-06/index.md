## Welcome to Module 6

Module 6 links financing decisions to investment decisions. Students estimate the cost of equity, after-tax cost of debt, capital structure weights, and WACC so they can evaluate whether projects earn enough to create value.

---

## Essential Question

> **How should a firm estimate the return it must earn on investments to create value?**

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

### Primary Outcome

#### LO4

**Evaluate a firm's cost of capital and capital investment projects based on sophisticated capital budgeting techniques.**

This module also draws on earlier or related course outcomes so students can connect individual finance tools into broader financial judgment.
