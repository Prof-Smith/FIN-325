## Welcome to Module 3

Module 3 develops valuation logic. Students learn how cash flows occurring at different points in time are compared, then apply that logic to bond pricing, yield, premium and discount bonds, and interest rate sensitivity.

---

## Essential Question

> **How do time, interest rates, and promised cash flows determine the value of bonds and other investments?**

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

### Primary Outcome

#### LO3

**Determine the time value of money, risk and return concepts, and value of a stock, bond, or other investment.**

### Integrated Prior Outcome

This module also draws on earlier or related course outcomes so students can connect individual finance tools into broader financial judgment.

- **LO2:** Financial performance affects credit quality and the interpretation of fixed-income risk.
