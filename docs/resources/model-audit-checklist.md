## Purpose

Use this checklist before submitting any Excel workbook, lab model, or capstone workbook.

<div class="fin-action-panel">
<strong>Core standard:</strong> A financial model should be transparent enough for another analyst to audit, update, and explain.
</div>

## Workbook Structure

- [ ] Workbook tabs are clearly named.
- [ ] Inputs are separated from formulas.
- [ ] Major outputs are easy to find.
- [ ] Units are labeled in headers.
- [ ] Sources are documented.
- [ ] The model includes enough explanation for another person to follow it.

## Formula Audit

- [ ] Formulas reference input cells instead of hardcoded assumptions.
- [ ] Formulas are copied consistently across years or scenarios.
- [ ] No formula errors appear, such as `#REF!`, `#DIV/0!`, `#VALUE!`, `#NAME?`, or `#N/A`.
- [ ] Totals sum the relevant range directly above or beside the total.
- [ ] Scenario formulas use the correct scenario inputs.
- [ ] WACC, discount rate, and growth assumptions are not buried in formulas.

## Professional Formatting

- [ ] Blue/yellow cells are student inputs.
- [ ] Black cells are formulas.
- [ ] Green cells link from another worksheet.
- [ ] Negative numbers appear in red parentheses.
- [ ] Zeros appear as dashes where appropriate.
- [ ] Rows and columns are wide enough to read.
- [ ] The workbook avoids clutter and unnecessary border lines.

## Interpretation Check

- [ ] The model answers a decision question.
- [ ] The key output is connected to the recommendation.
- [ ] Assumptions are stated clearly.
- [ ] Limitations are acknowledged.
- [ ] The final recommendation follows from the evidence.

<div class="fin-next-steps">
<strong>Before submitting:</strong> Open the workbook, review each tab from left to right, update the Dashboard, and make sure the final recommendation is consistent with the evidence.</div>
