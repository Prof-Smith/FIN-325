## Purpose

Students often know the calculation but struggle to explain what the calculation means. This guide shows the difference between weak finance writing and stronger professional analysis.

## Financial Health

<div class="fin-card-grid-2">
<div class="fin-example-card fin-weak">
<h3>Weak</h3>
<p>The company has a current ratio of 1.8 and ROE of 22%, so it is good.</p>
</div>
<div class="fin-example-card fin-strong">
<h3>Strong</h3>
<p>The company appears financially healthy because liquidity is adequate and return on equity is strong. However, the DuPont analysis should be used to determine whether ROE is driven by operating performance or by leverage.</p>
</div>
</div>

## Valuation

<div class="fin-card-grid-2">
<div class="fin-example-card fin-weak">
<h3>Weak</h3>
<p>The stock is good because the company is profitable.</p>
</div>
<div class="fin-example-card fin-strong">
<h3>Strong</h3>
<p>The company appears financially strong, but the stock does not appear clearly undervalued because the base-case valuation is close to the current market price and the conclusion is sensitive to the assumed growth rate.</p>
</div>
</div>

## Risk and Return

<div class="fin-card-grid-2">
<div class="fin-example-card fin-weak">
<h3>Weak</h3>
<p>The beta is 1.2, so the stock is risky.</p>
</div>
<div class="fin-example-card fin-strong">
<h3>Strong</h3>
<p>The beta of 1.2 suggests above-market systematic risk, which raises the required return. If the expected return does not exceed the CAPM required return, the stock may not compensate investors adequately for risk.</p>
</div>
</div>

## WACC

<div class="fin-card-grid-2">
<div class="fin-example-card fin-weak">
<h3>Weak</h3>
<p>The WACC is 9%, so the company should invest.</p>
</div>
<div class="fin-example-card fin-strong">
<h3>Strong</h3>
<p>The WACC of 9% represents the estimated hurdle rate for average-risk projects. Projects should create value only if expected returns exceed this cost of capital after accounting for forecast risk and scenario sensitivity.</p>
</div>
</div>

## Capstone Recommendation

<div class="fin-card-grid-2">
<div class="fin-example-card fin-weak">
<h3>Weak</h3>
<p>I recommend the stock because the company is successful.</p>
</div>
<div class="fin-example-card fin-strong">
<h3>Strong</h3>
<p>I would place the stock on a watchlist because the company appears financially healthy and strategically flexible, but the valuation does not provide a sufficient margin of safety under the base assumptions.</p>
</div>
</div>
