Finance is not about memorizing formulas. Finance is about making decisions when information is incomplete, uncertainty is unavoidable, and resources are limited.

Throughout this course, you will analyze real companies, build financial models, evaluate financial tradeoffs, and defend recommendations like a financial professional.

## What You Will Build

By the end of the course, you will have created a professional finance portfolio that may include:

- Financial statement analysis
- Ratio and DuPont analysis
- Time value of money models
- Bond and equity valuation work
- Risk and return analysis
- WACC and capital budgeting analysis
- Investment memos
- A final valuation recommendation

<div class="callout-finance">
<strong>Course operating principle:</strong> calculations matter, but interpretation and judgment create value.
