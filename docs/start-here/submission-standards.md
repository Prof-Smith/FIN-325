# Submission Standards

Professional finance work should be easy to read, easy to audit, and easy to defend.

## Spreadsheet standards

- Use clear labels.
- Separate inputs from calculations.
- Use formulas consistently.
- Format output tables professionally.
- Avoid unexplained hard-coded numbers.

## Written analysis standards

- State your conclusion clearly.
- Support claims with financial evidence.
- Explain why the evidence matters.
- Avoid unsupported opinions.

## Decision standard

A strong recommendation should answer three questions:

1. What do you recommend?
2. What evidence supports the recommendation?
3. What risks or limitations should the reader understand?
