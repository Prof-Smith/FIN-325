## Why This Site Exists

This site is designed to help you learn finance by doing finance.

You will use module pages to understand the concepts, labs to build evidence, investment memos to communicate decisions, and the capstone to synthesize the course into a final professional recommendation.

## What Good Work Looks Like

Good work does not stop at calculation.

Good work:

- States a clear decision
- Uses relevant evidence
- Explains what the evidence means
- Acknowledges assumptions and limitations
- Connects the analysis to a professional recommendation

<div class="decision-box">
<strong>Simple rule:</strong> If a number does not help a decision-maker understand the recommendation, explain why it matters or leave it out.</div>

## Recommended Habit

At the end of each major assignment, save your strongest evidence in a running capstone file.

Use this structure:

```text
Company
Financial Health Evidence
Valuation Evidence
Risk Evidence
Cost of Capital Evidence
Capital Allocation Evidence
Strategic Finance Evidence
Key Risks
Final Recommendation Notes
```

This will make the final capstone much easier to complete.
